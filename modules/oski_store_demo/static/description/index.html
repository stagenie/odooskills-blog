Invoice Reminder

Automatic follow-up on overdue customer invoices

Send a reminder to your customers as soon as an invoice goes past its due date, with a configurable delay and a fully editable email template.

Key features

Configurable delay

Choose how many days after the due date the first reminder is sent, then the cadence of the next ones.

Editable templates

Each reminder level uses a standard Odoo mail template you can adapt without writing code.

Full audit trail

Every reminder is logged in the invoice chatter, so the collection history stays auditable.
